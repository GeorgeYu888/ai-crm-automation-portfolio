# GoHighLevel Operating Rules

- Keep pipelines simple enough for a non-technical client to understand.
- Every stage should have a clear owner, next action and exit condition.
- Tags should support segmentation, automation and reporting, not become a dumping ground.
- New automations must be tested with sample contacts before being enabled for live leads.
- Client-facing workflows need plain-English training notes and screenshots or short instructions.
- Broken automations require a rollback plan, test run and human confirmation before reactivation.
